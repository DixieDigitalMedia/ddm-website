The Infrastructure Problem

Small organizations rely on scattered systems, personal email accounts, and one person who "knows where everything is." When that person leaves, chaos follows. We fix that.

The Bus Test

If your key person left tomorrow, what would you lose? Passwords, files, institutional memory? We build systems that survive leadership changes.

Rented vs. Owned

Facebook-only looks temporary. Professional organizations own their domain, control their narrative, and preserve their history on systems they control.

The Admin Burden

Small teams spend hours on routine admin—emails, meeting minutes, expense tracking. We provide AI assistance that handles the tedious work.
