Frequently Asked Questions

How long does setup take?

Digital Foundation typically takes 1-2 weeks. Office Suite setup takes 2-3 weeks including AI assistant configuration and training.

Do I need technical expertise?

No. We handle all the technical setup. You just need to use email and basic office tools. If you can send an email, you can use our systems.

What if I already have a website?

We can work with what you have. Sometimes we recommend starting fresh, but often we can improve your existing foundation and add the Office Suite services.

Can I upgrade later?

Absolutely. Many clients start with Digital Foundation and upgrade to Office Suite once they see the value. The upgrade is seamless.

What if I want to cancel Office Suite?

Office Suite is month-to-month. You keep your Google Account, your data, and your domain. We simply remove the AI assistant infrastructure and discontinue support services. Everything else stays with you.
