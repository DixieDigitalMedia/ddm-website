Frequently Asked Questions

Is the playbook really free?

Yes. We believe every heritage organization deserves access to this information. Download it, share it with your board, and put it to work.

What format is the playbook?

PDF format, readable on any device. You can print it, share it with your board, or keep it on your phone for reference.

Do I need technical expertise to implement the recommendations?

No. The playbook is written for non-technical leaders. If you can use email and basic office software, you can implement these systems. And if you need help, we're here.

Will you spam me after I download?

No. We'll send you the playbook and occasional helpful resources. You can unsubscribe anytime, and we never share your information.
