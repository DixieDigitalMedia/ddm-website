Our Story

Dixie Digital | Media was founded to solve a problem we saw everywhere: mission-driven organizations with powerful purposes but fragile digital foundations.

Robert Highbaugh, our founder, spent years working with heritage organizations, nonprofits, and community groups. He kept seeing the same scenario: a dedicated volunteer—often retired, always busy—suddenly became the "tech person" because they were the only one willing to figure it out.

These weren't IT professionals. They were historians, teachers, contractors, and community leaders who stepped up because someone had to. They managed websites they didn't build, email accounts they didn't create, and files scattered across personal laptops.

And then something would happen. The "tech person" would move away, get sick, or burn out. And the organization would discover that their digital presence—their connection to the world—was held together by one person's memory.

We created Dixie Digital | Media to change that.
